# Appointment Titles

It is used to disguised the level at which the respective appointment holders are held, although it has no security value.

1. SUNRAY - Commander
2. MOONBEAM - Chief of Staff
2. MANHOLE - Administration/Manpower/G1/S1 Staff Officer
3. MOLAR - QM/Log/G4/S4 Staff Officer

7. HOLDFAST - Engineer Representative

Note on Misuse of Appointment Titles:
One title is not to be used to qualify another, or as an adjective.

a. SUNRAY IRONSIDE should not be used.
b. Referring to tanks as IRONSIDES, and artillery support as SHELLDRAKE SUPPORT etc.
