# Learn 16 - Nicknames and Authentication

The proword for Authentication are AUTHENTICATE, I AUTHENTICATE, WRONG OUT.
(1) Nicknames are two words used by Signals for the purpose of activating five communication drills. They are (a) Changing of frequency, (b) Imposing of radio silence, (c) Breaking of radio silence, (d) Lifting of radio silence, (e) Closing down of a net.

(2) When the two-words Nicknames is activated, (a) First station is to authenticate, (b) All Stations are to readback the Nicknames so as to acknowledge and comply with the communication drills, (c) lastly, the initiating Station will ack with the Nicknames followed by OUT to indicate the start of the communication drill.

Example:
HULLO all stations DELTA FOXTROT, this is A00, BLUE SPADE OVER.
B19 wait... AUTHENTICATE Bravo Three OVER.
A00 wait... I AUTHENTICATE Eight Three OVER.
B19, roger BLUE SPADE OVER.
C29, BLUE SPADE OVER.
D39, BLUE SPADE OVER.
A00, BLUE SPADE now, OUT.

When the authentication is wrong, the initiating station will reply WRONG OUT.

NOTE: Authentication is also used when (1) Joining a Working Net, (2) On a suspicious station
